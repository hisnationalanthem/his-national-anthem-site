Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about commissions, memberships, free requests, payments, and bot use.

General Questions

This website is the central place for his_national_anthem commissions, memberships, free requests, upcoming bots, updates, and related information.

Where can I see upcoming bots?

Upcoming bots and the weekly posting schedule are available on the Upcoming Bots page.

Where can I browse existing bots?

The Bot Masterlist page will contain the searchable bot archive and organized series.

Paid Requests

Commission Questions

What kinds of commissions are available?

Available commission types include New Bots, Alt Bots, Graveyard Resurrections, Bot Remasters, Media Inspired Bots, and OC creation.

Can I add extra images?

Yes. Up to five additional images can be added to a commission for $1 CAD per image.

Can my commissioned bot be private?

Yes. Private use is available as a no-cost commission option.

How long will my commission take?

Turnaround information will be added once the final commission workflow and queue system are set.

Mama's Baby

Membership Questions

How much is the membership?

Mama's Baby is $10 CAD per month.

How many commission credits do members receive?

Members receive five commission credits each month.

Do unused commission credits roll over?

Yes. Unused credits roll over, with a maximum stored balance of twenty commission credits.

Can membership commission credits be donated?

Yes. Commission credits can be formally donated through the membership system.

Payment Questions

What payment methods are accepted?

Stripe and PayPal will be supported once checkout is connected.

What currency are prices listed in?

Commission and membership prices are listed in Canadian dollars.

Is there a currency converter?

Yes. A dedicated currency converter page is included in the website.

Free Requests

Request Questions

Are free requests the same as commissions?

No. Free requests are submitted through separate request forms and do not follow the paid commission workflow.

Is a Graveyard Request the same as a Graveyard Resurrection?

No. A Graveyard Request is part of the free request system, while a Graveyard Resurrection is a paid commission option.
